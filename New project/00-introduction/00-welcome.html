-Lauren Chambers, A Different Kind of Dark Energy: Placing Race and Gender in Physics

What is Modern Physics?

Modern physics covers physics ranging from relativity to gravitational waves. There is still an incredible amount that we do not know about the universe, so we will also expose you to current research areas along with the Big Questions of today.

The Modern Physics curriculum has traditionally been centered on Europe and heavily influenced by the contributions of white men. This focus stems from historical factors such as colonialism, the dissemination channels of science during the early 1900s, and persistent systemic racism and gender discrimination. As we explore the remarkable advancements in our understanding of the physical world, we will also delve into the cultural history of physics and highlight lesser-known figures who have significantly contributed to technological innovations and scientific discoveries.

Personal Reflection

Your personal reflection is an important component of your personal trajectory as a scientist. You will be graded only on completion, but please take the time to sincerely reflect on the following questions. I will use the collective responses to help me design better course materials in the future.

  1. Why did you decide to take this course?
  2. What are your personal goals for this course?
  3. How do you feel when you think about doing the work in this course?
  4. What are my biggest strengths in physics? Areas of improvement?
  5. Do you think science is objective?
  6. When experiences in science courses made you feel included? Excluded?
